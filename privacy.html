Data controller and contact

BirdQuiet is published by BirdQuiet Labs, which acts as data controller from Spain.

For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, write to birdquietlabs@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer because, given the kind of processing involved, the law does not require one.

Full focus (optional)

Full focus uses Android’s “Usage access” permission. Before asking for it, the app shows a notice explaining which data is used and why. It only switches on if you tap “I agree. Continue” and then grant the permission in Android’s settings.

With that permission, during a session BirdQuiet checks, including in the background, when the screen was turned on, locked or unlocked and which apps were in the foreground. It uses this to calculate your focus time and adjust the habitat’s stress and the chance of sightings. Android may provide events from a few minutes before the start so the app knows the initial state, but only the session itself is counted.

BirdQuiet does not read the content of other apps, your messages or your browsing history. Nor does it keep a list of the apps you opened: it only keeps the result, meaning the session’s quiet time and statistics.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by choosing basic mode, removing usage access in Android’s settings or resetting BirdQuiet. Withdrawing it does not affect processing carried out before. Basic mode, which does not need this permission, counts the time you spend on the focus screen or away from BirdQuiet.

Android permissions the app uses

  • Usage access: optional, only for full focus.
  • Notifications: optional, to show the timer and tell you when the session ends. What appears on the lock screen depends on your phone’s settings, and anyone who sees it can read it.
  • Foreground service: keeps the timer visible during a session you start. You can stop it from the app or from Android.
  • Vibration and widget: for alerts and to show the timer on your home screen.

BirdQuiet does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or your files, and has no permission to connect to the internet. Some Android libraries include technical permissions (such as briefly keeping the phone awake or checking whether a network is available) that give no access to personal data.

Who the data is shared with

No one. We do not sell data or share it with advertisers or other companies.

Google Play handles downloading and updating the app and may process its own data (for example, from your Google account, or crash reports if you have enabled them in Android) under its own privacy policy. That processing is Google’s responsibility, not BirdQuiet’s.

If you email us, your message passes through your email provider and ours (Gmail, by Google). Google may process that data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards set out in its terms, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android does not let other apps access. Because the app cannot connect to the internet, there is no transmission to intercept. BirdQuiet is set up to be excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backups and from device-to-device transfer, although this may vary by manufacturer.

We recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock, because anyone who unlocks it can see your journal and statistics.

Retention and deletion

Your progress stays on your phone until you delete it. In Settings you can clear the journal or reset BirdQuiet; these are separate actions and each asks you to confirm twice. Resetting deletes your progress and preferences and withdraws your consent to full focus. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android also deletes everything.

We do not offer cloud recovery, so anything you delete cannot be restored.

Minors

BirdQuiet is intended for teenagers aged 16 and 17 and for adults aged 18 and over. It is not meant for anyone under 16, who should not use it.

From 16 you can accept the full focus notice yourself, since in Spain people can consent to the processing of their own data from the age of 14. If you are a parent or guardian and believe someone under 16 is using BirdQuiet or has written to us, contact us and we will delete any message from them. Remember that the app’s data exists only on their phone and is deleted by resetting or uninstalling BirdQuiet.

BirdQuiet is a focus aid and a way to learn about wildlife. It is not a medical treatment or a parental-control tool.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to object to and restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time.

Because the app’s data exists only on your phone, you can view, change and delete it yourself in the app. For data you have sent us by email, write to birdquietlabs@gmail.com and we will reply within one month at most.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or with the supervisory authority in your country.

The game decides sightings with automatic rules, but these decisions have no legal effect on you and do not affect you significantly. We do not carry out profiling.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy policy. We do not receive those logs.

The site uses no cookies, ads, analytics or third-party resources: fonts and images are served from the site itself. If you change the language, your browser stores that choice in its local storage to remember it; you can delete it in your browser’s settings.
